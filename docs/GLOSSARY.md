# SkillBridge Project Glossary

This document provides concise, project-specific definitions for core concepts used across the SkillBridge codebase, product specifications, and documentation.

## Core Concepts & Entities

### Candidate Profile
The structured representation of a user's career qualifications extracted from their resume or manually inputted, including education, verified skills, existing projects, work experience, and certifications.

### Opportunity
A discrete employment, internship, apprenticeship, or government role listing tracked by SkillBridge, containing title, organization, description, location, deadline, and structured requirements.

### Opportunity Requirement
A specific constraint, skill, education level, or experience criterion demanded by an opportunity listing, categorized as either a hard eligibility gate or a soft skill requirement.

### Eligibility
The binary assessment of whether a candidate satisfies all mandatory statutory, academic, age, location, and workplace experience rules required by an opportunity.

### Hard Eligibility
A non-negotiable binary gate (e.g. required Bachelor's degree, minimum age, un-expired application deadline, mandatory work experience years). Failing a hard eligibility check disqualifies a candidate from being recommended for immediate application.

### Readiness
An explainable, multi-dimensional assessment score indicating how prepared a candidate is to pursue a specific opportunity, combining hard eligibility, semantic skill compatibility, evidence strength, and experience alignment.

### READY (🟢 READY)
The top readiness classification assigned to an opportunity when the candidate satisfies all hard eligibility gates and exhibits high skill and evidence compatibility (typically $\ge 80\%$). Recommended for immediate application.

### ALMOST READY (🟡 ALMOST READY)
The readiness classification assigned when a candidate satisfies all hard eligibility gates but possesses addressable skill or evidence gaps (typically $50\% - 79\%$). Recommended for preparation via targeted project work before applying.

### NOT READY (🔴 NOT READY)
The readiness classification assigned when a candidate fails one or more mandatory hard eligibility gates OR exhibits major missing core capabilities (typically $< 50\%$). Represents an aspirational role requiring long-term preparation.

## Gap Taxonomy

### Skill Gap
A gap occurring when a candidate completely lacks a technical, domain, or tool capability explicitly required by an opportunity. Can be resolved through learning and project implementation.

### Evidence Gap
A gap occurring when a candidate claims or possesses a skill, but lacks demonstrable portfolio proof, public code repositories, or project evidence to substantiate the claim to employers.

### Experience Gap
A gap occurring when an opportunity explicitly demands formal full-time workplace experience that the candidate does not yet possess. Cannot be satisfied merely by completing an academic portfolio project.

### Hard Eligibility Gap
A gap occurring when a candidate fails a non-negotiable statutory or explicit constraint (e.g. missing degree, age limit, passed deadline). Cannot be resolved by portfolio projects.

## Intelligence & Matching Concepts

### Semantic Skill Matching
The technique of evaluating candidate capabilities against opportunity requirements using vector embeddings and similarity metrics to recognize related terminology without declaring non-identical skills fully equivalent.

### Evidence Provenance
The lineage metadata recorded for candidate skills and opportunity requirements, tracking the exact text snippet, document section, and source context from which a claim or rule was extracted.

### Confidence
An explicit rating (High, Medium, Low, Unknown) assigned to AI-extracted information indicating how directly the source evidence supports the extracted candidate claim or opportunity rule.

### Opportunity Discovery
The product capability allowing candidates to search, view, and explore all relevant opportunities across all readiness tiers, ensuring aspirational roles remain visible.

### Opportunity Recommendation
The intelligent prioritization mechanism that ranks and highlights specific opportunities candidates should focus on pursuing now based on current eligibility and readiness.

### Project Recommendation
A customized project specification generated by SkillBridge designed to close specific skill and evidence gaps for high-priority target opportunities while reusing the candidate's existing strengths.

### Project Evidence
Tangible deliverables produced by completing a project (e.g. GitHub repository, live URL, dataset script, interactive dashboard) that substantiate a candidate's skill claim.

### Reassessment
The engine operation that re-evaluates a candidate's profile against the opportunity landscape after simulated or actual completion of a recommended project.

### Newly Reachable Opportunity
An opportunity whose readiness classification upgrades (e.g. from 🟡 ALMOST READY to 🟢 READY) following the successful completion of a gap-targeted project.

### Readiness Delta ($\Delta \text{Readiness}$)
The calculated numerical score increase in candidate readiness expected or achieved upon completing a specific recommended project.
